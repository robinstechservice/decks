前言 Intro

EN. With each new season comes another YC Demo Day. On Tuesday, the Spring 2026 cohort was unveiled, filled with defense tech, robotics, AI infrastructure and developer tools, and of course, AI agents. 中. 每一季都有新的 YC Demo Day。週二，2026 春季梯隊亮相，滿是國防科技、機器人、AI 基礎建設與開發者工具——當然，還有 AI agent。

EN. TechCrunch spoke to eight investors to determine which ones were the hottest companies in this batch. This list consists primarily of companies flagged by at least two investors as the buzziest in the batch. 中. TechCrunch 訪問了八位投資人，找出這一梯最熱門的公司。這份名單主要由「至少兩位投資人都點名」最受熱議的公司組成。

EN. That excitement translated into larger rounds and massive price tags. This batch had at least two startups fetching valuations of $175 million or more. Investors were also clearly willing to pay a premium for proven, repeat founders. 中. 這股熱度轉化成更大的募資輪與驚人的估值。這一梯至少有兩家估值達 1.75 億美元以上。投資人也明顯願意為「有過成功紀錄的連續創業者」付溢價。

1. 9 Mothers — AI 反無人機系統 AI-powered counter-drone systems

EN. The Russia-Ukraine conflict is showing just how lethal small drones are, now accounting for roughly 80% of casualties. 9 Mothers claims it developed a more “affordable” robot that can track and kill drones traveling at 60 mph. Founded in 2024, it has booked $1.6M in sales, with a single contract expected to expand to $35M later this year, and promises a pipeline of $1 billion in contracts. VCs invested at a valuation upward of $200M — potentially one of the most valuable in YC history. 中. 俄烏衝突顯示小型無人機有多致命，如今約佔傷亡的 80%。9 Mothers 宣稱做出更「平價」、能追蹤並擊落時速 60 英里無人機的機器人。2024 年成立，已有 160 萬美元銷售額，單一合約預計今年稍後擴大到 3,500 萬美元，並向投資人承諾 10 億美元的合約 pipeline。創投以逾 2 億美元估值投資——可能是 YC 史上最高估值之一。

2. Arga Labs — AI agent 測試的數位分身環境 Digital twin environments for testing AI agents

EN. AI helps engineers generate code far faster, but traditional testing sandboxes can’t be created fast enough to keep up. Arga Labs instantly spins up “digital twins” of a company’s software, letting AI agents safely test code before it reaches production. 中. AI 讓工程師更快產出程式碼，但傳統測試沙箱來不及跟上。Arga Labs 能即時建立公司軟體的「數位分身」，讓 AI agent 在程式碼上線前安全測試。

3. Adialante — 早期癌症篩檢的行動 MRI 診所 Mobile MRI clinics for early cancer detection

EN. Early detection saves lives, but MRI machines are too expensive to screen everyone. Adialante designed a compact MRI unit transportable in a small truck, bringing it to clinics and charging $250 a scan, aiming to turn MRI from symptomatic-only into routine annual screening. 中. 早期偵測能救命，但 MRI 機器太貴、無法普篩。Adialante 設計出可用小貨車載運的緊湊型 MRI，帶到各診所、每次掃描收費 250 美元，目標是把 MRI 從「只給有症狀者」變成「每年例行篩檢」。

4. Complir — 實體商品的合規管理 Compliance management for physical products

EN. Shipping physical goods across borders is complicated (EU translations, country-specific labeling). Complir builds AI agents that help businesses manage compliance, risk, and regulatory changes, and generate the documentation and product labels needed for international shipping. 中. 跨境運送實體商品很複雜（歐盟翻譯、各國標籤規範）。Complir 打造 AI agent，協助企業管理合規、風險與法規變動，並產出國際運送所需的文件與產品標籤。

5. Dispatch — 把太空製造的產品安全送回地球 Satellites that return space-manufactured products to Earth

EN. Space’s microgravity and near-vacuum are great for manufacturing certain pharma, semiconductors, and 3D-printed tissues. Dispatch builds vehicles that withstand the heat to return those products, designed to be refurbished and reused over and over. 中. 太空的微重力與近真空很適合製造特定藥品、半導體與 3D 列印組織。Dispatch 打造能耐熱、把這些產品送回地球的載具，且設計成可翻修、反覆重複使用。

6. Lightsprint — 讓非工程師免寫程式就能上線功能 Let non-engineers ship features without code

EN. Lightsprint lets anyone build and ship production features. A PM explains what they want, picks from visual options, and a Lightsprint AI agent builds it; then an engineer reviews, approves, and merges before shipping. 中. Lightsprint 讓任何人都能打造並上線正式功能。產品經理說明需求、從視覺選項中挑選，Lightsprint 的 AI agent 替他做出來；最後由工程師審查、核准、合併後才正式上線。

7. Ploy — 自動建站 + 行銷成長 Automates website building and marketing growth

EN. Shortly after being listed, Ploy announced a $27M seed led by First Round and Y Combinator. Founded by Bryant Chou (co-founder/former CTO of Webflow, last valued at $4B), Ploy auto-generates landing pages, writes marketing copy, launches campaigns, and continuously refines content to accelerate inbound growth — promising to eliminate the need for a large marketing team. 中. 才剛被點名，Ploy 就宣布由 First Round 與 Y Combinator 領投的 2,700 萬美元種子輪。創辦人 Bryant Chou 是 Webflow（最近估值 40 億美元）的共同創辦人兼前 CTO。Ploy 自動產出落地頁、寫行銷文案、發起活動，並持續優化內容以加速 inbound 成長——承諾讓你不再需要一支龐大的行銷團隊。

8. Sazabi — 找出並自動修復軟體問題的 AI 平台 AI platform to find and fix software problems

EN. Founded by Sherwood Callaway (repeat YC founder, ex-a16z scout, Brex, 11x). Sazabi integrates with Slack, performs log analysis to determine why something broke in production, and lets users generate and submit a fix in one click. 中. 創辦人 Sherwood Callaway 是連續 YC 創業者（曾任 a16z scout、Brex、11x）。Sazabi 整合 Slack、做日誌分析以判斷正式環境為何出錯，並讓使用者一鍵產生並提交修復。

9. Silmaril — AI 資安基礎建設 AI security infrastructure

EN. As VCs fund countless AI-agent companies, Silmaril ensures agents can’t be hacked via “prompt injection” — its public-facing parts can’t be manipulated via prompts, emails, or documents. Silmaril’s agents autonomously probe for new threats and retrain the firewall to develop immunity. 中. 在創投大舉投資 AI agent 之際，Silmaril 確保 agent 不被「提示注入」攻破——對外的部分不會被惡意提示、郵件或文件操控。它的 agent 會自主偵測新威脅，並重新訓練防火牆對其產生「免疫力」。

10. Superset — 同時跑 100+ 個 coding agent 的平台 Run 100+ coding agents simultaneously

EN. As coding agents proliferate, they need management in one space. Superset lets developers launch 100+ coding agents at once; any CLI agent (Claude, Cursor) runs in its own workspace with no conflicts, and it opens in any IDE (VS Code, Cursor). 中. 隨著 coding agent 暴增，需要一個地方統一管理。Superset 讓開發者一次啟動 100+ 個 coding agent；任何 CLI agent（Claude、Cursor）都在各自工作區內執行、互不衝突，也能在任何 IDE（VS Code、Cursor）開啟。

11. Tasklet — 執行任務的 AI agent A task-performing AI agent

EN. Tasklet connects to work apps’ APIs (Slack, Outlook, Google Drive) and performs tasks via natural language — sorting emails, pulling reports. It runs continuously (works even if you close the tab), writes and runs its own code, and builds interfaces. It positions itself as a place to give commands rather than opening apps individually. 中. Tasklet 串接各種工作軟體的 API（Slack、Outlook、Google Drive），用自然語言執行任務——整理郵件、拉報表。它持續運作（關掉分頁也照做）、會自己寫並執行程式碼、還能建介面。它把自己定位成「你下指令的地方」，而非逐一打開各個應用程式。

Eos 一句編按

這份名單對羅賓斯科技有兩個直接訊號：① AI agent 已是創投主流下注（11 家裡多家是 agent 應用：Tasklet/Superset/Complir/Lightsprint/Arga/Silmaril），印證我們把 agent 放進業務流程的方向；② 其中 Complir（跨境合規 agent）、Silmaril（agent 防提示注入資安）兩家，分別對位我們最關心的「合規」與「agent 安全」——值得列為標竿觀察。
