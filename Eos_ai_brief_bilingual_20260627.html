中英對照｜LLMs are eroding my software engineering career and I don’t know what to do

註：原文為英文部落格（the human in the loop，2026-06-06）。以下為對照閱讀版，依原文論點逐段整理；引號內為原文逐字句（verbatim），其餘為忠實轉述與翻譯，非原文整段照抄。原文連結見文末。

EN. The author, a senior software engineer, argues that LLMs are systematically dismantling the specialized expertise that used to make senior engineers valuable and hard to replace. 中. 作者是一位資深軟體工程師，他主張 LLM 正在系統性地拆解「讓資深工程師有價值、難以取代」的那套專業能力。

EN. Pillar one is domain knowledge. He spent years building deep expertise in finance and payments, trusting that specialization meant job security. But models trained on existing documentation and blog posts can now synthesize that knowledge instantly, needing only light steering. 中. 第一根支柱是領域知識。他花多年鑽研金融與支付，相信「越專精越安全」。但吃下既有文件與部落格的模型，如今只要稍加引導，就能即時把那套知識合成出來。

EN. As he puts it: “I have no domain expertise that another Sr. engineer steering an LLM cannot match.” And: “all the knowledge I have accumulated over the years…was becoming useless.” 中. 他寫道：「我沒有任何一個領域專長，是另一個資深工程師駕馭 LLM 後做不到的。」以及：「我這些年累積的所有知識……正在變得沒用。」

EN. Pillar two is debugging and distributed systems—long seen as an irreplaceable human skill. He says LLMs now resolve roughly 90% of bugs autonomously, including hard race conditions; issues that once took two days of investigation are handled by models wired up with MCPs and monitoring tools like DataDog. 中. 第二根支柱是除錯與分散式系統，一向被當成機器取代不了的人類技能。他說 LLM 現在能自主解掉約九成的 bug，連棘手的競態條件都行；過去要查兩天的問題，搭配 MCP 與 DataDog 這類監控工具的模型自己就能搞定。

EN. Pillar three is code quality and architecture—the last thing he thought would hold. But industry priorities are shifting: “code organization is less important,” and even “C or D-grade codebases” are now acceptable, because rewriting is cheap. 中. 第三根支柱是程式品質與架構，他原以為這是最後守得住的。但產業優先順序在變：「程式碼組織沒那麼重要了」，連「C 級或 D 級的程式碼」現在都能被接受，因為重寫的成本很低。

EN. The deepest cut is an economic one: “if everyone is a generalist, the price of a generalist falls.” AI’s real effect is not replacing one person but flattening the difference between an expert and a capable newcomer—and when difference disappears, so does bargaining power. 中. 最深的一刀是經濟學的：「如果每個人都是通才，通才的價碼就會崩。」AI 真正的作用不是取代某個人，而是抹平「專家」與「有能力的新人」之間的差距——而當差異消失，議價能力也跟著消失。

EN. He admits his own job is currently secure. What he fears is the long run: with each moat filled in, he can only keep migrating toward ever-rarer specializations, and he is not sure he can outrun it. He ends without an answer—just honesty: he doesn’t know what to do. 中. 他坦承自己目前工作穩定，怕的是長期：護城河一條條被填平後，他只能不斷往越來越稀有的專精領域遷徙，而且不確定自己逃得夠不夠快。文章結尾他沒給答案，只有誠實：他不知道該怎麼辦。
